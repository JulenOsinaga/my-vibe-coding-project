# Show & Swap Notes

> Module 2 · Validation. Put the prototype in front of a peer. Their reaction is the data.

## What I showed

A profile example to validate certain feature improvements focused on improving the trust on zero-reviews providers.

## What my partner understood (in their words)

I thought you were trying to validate features that would help sellers without reviews earn trust to push up their sales. I think it was very clear. I loved the result too, where it asked to iterate based on not meeting the requirement

## Where they got confused / pushed back

If this fails, I don't think it means that trust is not the barrier. I think it is established that trust is based on the fact that people prefer to shop from sellers that have reviews. What I think it will prove is that these are not the levers to improve trust and you need to re-iterate on what those levers are

## What I'll change as a result

I need to go deeper on the real levers that improve trust
