## The build

- **What I built:** A custom ceramics provider page to improve buyers trust.
- **Tool used:** Lovable
- **Shareable link:** https://trustful-spotlight.lovable.app

## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** The purpose of the page is to make potential customers to have faith on a company or product. It helps to build trust.
- **What confused them:** The check displayed for the IN REVIEW state could be confusing for the customer. It may be clearer to visually differentiate checks in the REVIEWED state from those in the IN REVIEW state.
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** Customers are not fully confident in the long-term viability of the company or its product.
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** Overall, the idea was well understood. There were some interesting suggestions for improvement, but the purpose of the prototype was clear.
